# YouTube upload kit — Insight Engine trailer

Claude cannot upload to YouTube: no authorised connector in this environment, and the
only browser path that reaches a signed-in Google account caps uploads at 10 MB (this
file is well over that). Uploading is a manual step - same constraint documented in
`examples/ltx25-optionseducator/youtube/upload-kit.md` for the master trailer.

## How to upload

1. Go to **studio.youtube.com**, signed in to the Options Educator channel.
2. **Create → Upload videos**, drag in
   `~/LTX-Renders/trailers/insight-engine/insight_engine_trailer.mp4`.
3. Paste the title and description below.
4. No thumbnail prepared yet - pick a strong frame from the "Confirm & Start" beat
   (~20s in) if you want a custom one, or let YouTube auto-select.
5. Audience: **"No, it's not made for kids."**
6. Visibility: your call - Unlisted first is the safe way to check it on the platform.
7. Add to the **Trailers** playlist.

## Description

Given a real scenario - an upcoming earnings event - the Insight Engine asks you to
choose a strategy and shows you exactly what you're signing up for before you commit.
Pick Iron Condor and you see its payoff curve drawn live, the range it profits in
($90-$110 in this scenario), and a plain-language read on when it works ("low
movement is expected") - then confirm to start the simulation.

This is the actual simulator, not a mockup.
