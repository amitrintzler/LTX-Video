# YouTube upload kit — Options Chain trailer

Claude cannot upload to YouTube: no authorised connector in this environment, and the
only browser path that reaches a signed-in Google account caps uploads at 10 MB (this
file is well over that). Uploading is a manual step - same constraint documented in
`examples/ltx25-optionseducator/youtube/upload-kit.md` for the master trailer.

## How to upload

1. Go to **studio.youtube.com**, signed in to the Options Educator channel.
2. **Create → Upload videos**, drag in
   `~/LTX-Renders/trailers/options-chain/options_chain_trailer.mp4`.
3. Paste the title and description below.
4. No thumbnail prepared yet - pick a strong frame from the chain-grid beat (~22s in)
   if you want a custom one, or let YouTube auto-select.
5. Audience: **"No, it's not made for kids."**
6. Visibility: your call - Unlisted first is the safe way to check it on the platform.
7. Add to the **Trailers** playlist.

## Description

Every strike, every Greek, repriced live. Options Educator's Options Chain tool shows
Black-Scholes pricing (bid, ask, delta, gamma, theta, vega) across the full strike
ladder as you move the underlying price, implied volatility, risk-free rate, and
expiry (7d/14d/30d/60d) - with in-the-money calls and puts and the at-the-money row
colour-coded so the shape of the chain is obvious at a glance.

This is the actual tool, not a mockup - drag a slider and watch every number update.

Part of Options Educator: https://gameofoptions.netlify.app

Educational purposes only. Not financial advice.
