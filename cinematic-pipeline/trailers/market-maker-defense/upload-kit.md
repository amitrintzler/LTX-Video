## Title

Market Maker Defense — Survive the Opening Bell

## Description

Market Makers provide liquidity by taking the other side of trades - and that creates risk. In Market Maker Defense, you accept incoming orders to earn commissions while balancing the scale: if your net delta goes positive, accept bearish trades to hedge; if it goes negative, accept bullish trades. Let gamma run too high and your delta swings wildly. The concept is explained before you're tested on it, then it's real-time pressure to stay liquid.

This is the actual mission, not a mockup.
