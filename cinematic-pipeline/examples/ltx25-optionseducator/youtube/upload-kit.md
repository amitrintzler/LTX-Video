# YouTube upload kit — Options Educator trailer

Everything below is ready to copy and paste. Claude cannot upload to YouTube: there is
no authorised YouTube connector in this environment, and the only browser path that
reaches a signed-in Google account caps file uploads at 10 MB. Uploading is a manual step.

## How to upload

1. Go to **studio.youtube.com** and sign in to the Options Educator channel.
2. **Create → Upload videos**, then drag in the final MP4.
3. Paste the title and description below.
4. Set the thumbnail to `thumbnail.jpg` from this folder.
5. Audience: **"No, it's not made for kids."**
6. Visibility: your call — Unlisted first is the safe way to check it on the platform.

## Description

Most options education hands you a library. Options Educator gives you the next step.

Options City is a live trading sandbox where the map *is* the curriculum. Walk Gamma
Street, Theta Path and IV Crush Alley. Learn chain literacy in Old Town, volatility
crush in Volatility Heights, and the Greeks on Pantheon Row. Read the market, pick the
play, execute, hold, and close for P&L.

Inside:
- One clear path — study a lesson, then apply it in a guided simulator drill before you advance
- Storybook lessons that make concepts stick: The Melting Ice Cream Shop teaches time decay, Goldilocks and the Three Strikes teaches strike selection
- Daily stories, animations, video, podcasts and market news
- An arcade that keeps growing — Market Maker Defense, Risk Ladder, Strategy Builder
- A curriculum spanning foundations, technicals, fundamentals, the Greeks, strategies and risk

Start your learning path: https://gameofoptions.netlify.app

Available in English and Hebrew, with more languages in progress.

Educational purposes only. Not financial advice.
